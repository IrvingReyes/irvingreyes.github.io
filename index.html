Understanding the system as a whole

«The whole is greater than the sum of its parts.»

I started in networks, servers, and databases, then moved into the cloud, DevOps, and software development. Along the way I realized that good solutions don’t live in a single layer: they depend on how every part works together.

That’s why I’ve always tried to understand the full picture: the business requirements, the infrastructure that runs the software, security and data integrity, the connections between systems, and the design of the solution itself.

In practice, I take ownership and accountability of projects end to end: from tuning PostgreSQL queries and deploying with containers and Odoo.sh, to building custom Odoo modules and modern OWL interfaces that solve real business problems.

AI has been a force multiplier across all of these skills, helping me research, build, document, and automate faster without losing sight of the bigger picture.
